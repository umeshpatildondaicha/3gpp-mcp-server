Priority roadmap (ordered by measured ROI)

  1. Release / technology-aware ranking ~+4–5 TOP-1 — closes his release-awareness lead and fixes our worst topic (LTE 1/8).
  2. Owning-spec tie-break — recovers the 2 regressions from the last round (AMF-N2, NSSI), where a non-gold spec flipped to #1 while gold sat at rank 2.
  3. Cross-spec procedure-flow tool — his headline feature, but for us it's packaging (retrieval already 8/9): high product value, low retrieval risk.
  4. Confidence gate + vector rescue — done carefully — our data shows a naive threshold regresses; needs the secondary-check design, measured on the harness.
  5. Clause-level chunking, then intent routing / self-validation — larger or robustness-oriented; sequence after the above.
